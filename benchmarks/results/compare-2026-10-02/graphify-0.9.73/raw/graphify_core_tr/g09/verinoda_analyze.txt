38 Honors the ``GRAPHIFY_MAX_GRAPH_BYTES`` environment variable so users with
39 large codebases can raise the limit without editing source. The value may
40 be plain bytes (``671088640``) or carry an ``MB`` / ``GB`` suffix
41 (``640MB``, ``2GB`` — case-insensitive, binary multipliers: ``MB`` is
42 1024*1024 and ``GB`` is 1024*1024*1024, i.e. MiB / GiB).
